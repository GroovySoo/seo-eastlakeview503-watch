### 2. Settle the Search Console question, once (10 minutes)

This is a **verification task, not a fire.** Do not read it as "the page is missing from Google" —
we do not know that, and our search tooling cannot tell us. Only Search Console can.

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console and pick the `eastlakeview503.relahq.com`
   property.
2. Paste this into the search bar at the very top of the page and press Enter:

```
https://eastlakeview503.relahq.com/
```

3. Wait for it to finish, and write down **exactly** which headline it gives you:
   - **"URL is on Google"** → good, the page is indexed. Our problem is strength, not presence, and
     action 1 is the entire job. This is the outcome I expect.
   - **"URL is not on Google"** → click **Request Indexing**, and write down the reason shown
     underneath it.
   - **"URL is available to Google"** → click **Request Indexing**.
4. Then in the left-hand menu open **Indexing → Pages** and write down the number next to
   "Not indexed" and the top reason listed.

Paste that headline and reason into Slack. Whatever it says, this item closes after this week — I
am not carrying an unanswerable question a third time.
