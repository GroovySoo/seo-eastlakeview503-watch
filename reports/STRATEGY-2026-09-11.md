## 2. Top 3 actions this week

### 1. Find out what Google actually thinks of this page (10 minutes, highest impact)

Everything else is guesswork until this is done.

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console and pick the `eastlakeview503.relahq.com` property.
2. Paste this into the search bar at the very top of the page and press Enter:

```
https://eastlakeview503.relahq.com/
```

3. Wait for it to finish. Read the headline it gives you and note **exactly** which of these it says:
   - **"URL is on Google"** → the page is indexed; our problem is strength, not presence. Go
     straight to action 2 and treat it as the whole job.
   - **"URL is not on Google"** → click **Request Indexing**, then write down any reason it shows
     underneath (e.g. "Discovered – currently not indexed", "Crawled – currently not indexed").
   - **"URL is available to Google"** but not yet indexed → click **Request Indexing**.
4. Then in the left menu open **Indexing → Pages** and note the number next to "Not indexed" and the
   top reason listed.

Paste the headline and that reason into the Slack channel. Next week's memo depends on it, and it
decides whether the sitemap chase in action 3 matters or not.
